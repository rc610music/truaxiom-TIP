# TIP-0001.1 — The Organizational Brain

## Status
Draft v0.1

## Purpose
The Organizational Brain is the central intelligence layer of the TruaXiom Intelligence Platform (TIP). Rather than acting as a chatbot or isolated AI assistant, it maintains a living understanding of an organization.

## Core Responsibilities
- Understand the organization's mission, vision, products, services, brand, customers, and workflows.
- Maintain relationships between knowledge rather than isolated documents.
- Provide context to every intelligence module before decisions are made.
- Continuously learn from approved actions and measurable outcomes.

## Foundational Principle
TIP does not begin with prompts. It begins with organizational understanding.

## Initial Core Domains
- Organization Profile
- Brand DNA
- Products & Services
- Projects
- Knowledge Assets
- Content
- Workflows
- Customers
- Metrics
- Connected Systems
